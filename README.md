💄 Sephora Beauty Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Sephora's beauty product catalog to uncover insights into product categories, pricing strategies, customer engagement, and product popularity.

Using Power BI and Power Query, I developed an interactive dashboard to explore 8,495+ beauty products and understand how different product categories and pricing segments perform in terms of customer engagement and ratings.

The dashboard transforms raw product data into meaningful business insights that can support retail decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze product distribution across different beauty categories.
- Understand pricing patterns and identify popular price segments.
- Explore customer engagement through wishlist likes.
- Evaluate product ratings across pricing categories.
- Identify popular products and customer preferences.
- Present key findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Tool| Purpose
Power BI| Dashboard development and visualization
Power Query| Data cleaning and transformation
CSV| Dataset storage and input

📊 Dataset Overview

- Total Products: 8,495+
- Domain: Beauty & Cosmetics Retail
- Data Format: CSV
- File: "product_info.csv"

The dataset contains product-level information used to analyze categories, prices, ratings, and customer wishlist engagement.

📈 Key Business Insights

1. Category Analysis

- Skincare and Makeup together account for over 60% of the total product catalog.
- These categories represent a major share of Sephora's product assortment.

2. Pricing Analysis

- Mid-range products priced between $30 and $70 represent 44.3% of the catalog.
- This price segment also shows strong customer ratings.

3. Customer Engagement

- Popular cult-favorite products receive over 1 million wishlist loves.
- Wishlist engagement highlights products attracting significant customer interest.

4. Product Popularity

- Wishlist likes and customer ratings provide useful indicators for understanding product popularity and customer preferences.
